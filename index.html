How to play

Goal

Score 3 points. You score a point each time you knock out one of your opponent's creatures, including creatures on their bench. If neither player reaches 3 points by turn 30, the higher score wins.

Your board

One active creature does the fighting. Up to three bench creatures wait behind it. Retreating swaps the active creature with one from the bench and costs that creature's retreat cost in energy.

Your turn

  • Play one item card
  • Play Stage 1 creatures to empty bench slots
  • Evolve creatures, from turn 3 onwards
  • Attach one energy to any creature you control. Its type (Mystic, Wind, Celestial or Mechanic) is shown on the button before you attach it: one of your deck's energy types, picked at random if the deck uses several
  • Use abilities — these must come before attacking. A creature whose ability is ready has a flashing rainbow border: tap it to see and use the ability
  • Retreat once, or attack, which ends your turn. The creature you retreat into can't be retreated again that turn (moves and abilities that switch creatures still work)

Energy types

Each letter in a move's cost is one energy: M Mystic, W Wind, C Celestial, K Mechanic, and N any type. A cost of MN needs 1 Mystic plus 1 of anything, so a creature holding 2 Celestial energy can't use it. Retreat costs accept any type. Each deck chooses its energy types in the deck builder.

Going first

A coin flip decides who goes first. To make up for moving first, that player does not draw and cannot attack on turn 1. They can still attach energy and play creatures. The other player draws and may attack on their first turn as normal.

Evolution

  • Not before turn 3
  • Not on a creature you played this turn
  • Not twice on the same creature in one turn
  • Damage, energy and status carry over to the new form
  • When an evolved creature is knocked out, every card in its chain goes to the discard pile

Deck building

  • Only cards you own, and no more copies than you own
  • Exactly 20 cards, at most 2 copies of any one card
  • At least one Stage 1 creature
  • Creatures of any types can be mixed. Pick the deck's energy types to match: a creature whose moves need an energy type the deck doesn't use gets a warning, but the deck can still be saved
  • Accessory cards are attached to one of your creatures of their type, one per turn
  • Cards from different sets can be mixed freely

QA mode: choose both decks

Pick any deck for either side, including the AI presets and test decks built from any card. QA games are not counted in stats and never earn credits.

🌐 Play online

ℹ️ How to play

🎮 What is this game?

Collect creature cards from the Elemental Awakening set (and later Galactic Adventures), build decks from the cards you own, and battle the AI. Draw cards with credits, buy specific ones in the Store with gems, complete missions and play the daily mini-games for more credits and gems.

🗂️ What each section does
  • ⚔️ Play: your decks, the deck builder, and games against the AI.
  • 📚 Card Dex: every card in a set and how many of each you own.
  • 🎰 Draw a Card: spend 1 credit for a random card from an unlocked set.
  • 🛒 Store: buy any specific card with gems.
  • 📋 Missions: objectives that pay credits and gems.
  • 🏅 Achievements: tasks for every single card, with credits, gems and promo cards as rewards.
  • 📅 Daily Challenges: three challenges a day (Easy, Medium, Hard) with deck rules to follow, for credits and cards.
  • 🐉 Raid Bosses: beat a boss deck to win the full-art version of its card.
  • 🎮 Mini-games: Spin the Wheel, Guess the Number and Memory Game, each free once a day for credits and gems. Each game shows whether you've played it today and what you earned.
  • 📈 Collection: your progress through each set.
  • 🏆 Stats: games played, won and lost against the AI, and your online games and win rate.
  • 💾 Save data: download and import backups, or move your save to another device.
🪙 Currency and draw odds
  • Credits: capped at 12, 1 comes back every hour (even while the game is closed), 1 per card draw.
  • Gems (💜): no cap, start with 5, earned from mini-games and missions, spent in the Store.

Draw odds: 🟢 Common 55% · 🔵 Uncommon 28% · ⭐ Rare 13% · 💎 Epic 4%.

Store prices: 🟢 Common 1 · 🔵 Uncommon 5 · ⭐ Rare 10 · 💎 Epic 20 gems.

⚔️ Decks and games against the AI
  • A deck is 20 cards you own: one main type plus any Neutral and Item cards, at least one Stage 1 creature, at most 2 copies of a card (and no more than you own).
  • Each deck also picks its energy types (Mystic, Wind, Celestial, Mechanic). It starts as the deck's main type. Pick more than one and each turn's energy is one of them at random; you see which before attaching it.
  • The AI plays one of its own preset decks, picked at random: Mystic, Wind, Celestial, Mechanic and Neutral decks. Once you unlock Galactic Adventures, 14 more AI decks built around that set join the pool.
  • Whoever goes first doesn't draw and can't attack on turn 1. Your Stats show your win rate going first and going second.
  • Each win earns 1 credit, up to 3 a day. If your credits are full when you start, a win earns nothing, and the game warns you first.
  • Leaving a game before it ends, or closing the page mid-game, counts as a loss.
📅 Daily Challenges
  • Every day at midnight three new challenges appear: Easy, Medium and Hard. Each has its own AI deck, built from strong cards of the sets you have unlocked, and the same deck all day.
  • Each challenge has rules for your deck, picked at random for the day: for example no cards of a type, no items, only cards from one set, only creatures that evolve, only 1 copy of each card. Easy has 1 rule, Medium 2 and Hard 3 tougher ones. The rules always leave a deck you can build from your own cards.
  • Easy needs 1 win. Medium needs 2 wins in a row and Hard 3 in a row; losing, drawing or leaving resets the streak.
  • Rewards: Easy 🪙 1 credit. Medium 🪙 1 credit and a card (Uncommon or better) from a random unlocked set. Hard 🪙 2 credits, a 50% chance of 💜 1 gem and a card (Uncommon or better) from the set you choose.
  • Rewards wait on the page until you claim them; the credits need room under the 12-credit cap. Challenge wins also count as normal wins, including the daily win credits.
🌐 Playing online against a friend
  • On the Play page, tap 🌐 Play online and pick your deck. One player taps Make a room and shares the 4-letter code; the other types it and taps Join.
  • Each turn has 60 seconds. When the time runs out, the turn ends (an open choice is answered for you).
  • A win pays 1 credit, sharing the same limit of 3 win credits a day as games against the AI. Leaving counts as a loss; if the other player leaves or disconnects for long enough, you win.
  • Online games have their own stats (games played, win rate) and their own missions. They don't count towards the card achievements.
  • If the online service is busy or at its free limit, Play online says so instead of starting. If your own connection drops for too long, or the two devices ever disagree about the board, the game stops and doesn't count.
🔒 Unlocking Galactic Adventures

Galactic Adventures unlocks once you own 90% of the non-promo Elemental Awakening cards (63 of 69). Promo cards don't count. Until then its cards can't be drawn, bought or used in decks. Once unlocked, choose which set to draw from.

🎡 Spin the Wheel
  • Once per day, only with 7 or fewer credits (you could win up to 5 more, and credits cap at 12).
  • 10 segments, alternating "No Win" and a prize: +1, +3 or +5 credits, or +1/+2 gems.
🎯 Guess the Number
  • Once per day, only with 7 or fewer credits.
  • Guess a number from 1 to 50. 3 or fewer guesses wins 5 credits, 4-5 wins 3, 6-7 wins 2, 8+ wins 1.
  • 50% chance of a bonus gem.
  • Once you start, you can't leave the page until you guess correctly.
🧠 Memory Game
  • Once per day, only with 7 or fewer credits.
  • Find all 8 pairs on the 4×4 board.
  • Reward: 50% chance of +2 credits, 40% +3, 10% +5, plus a 50% chance of a bonus gem.
📋 Missions
  • Collect cards (overall, by rarity, by type, with an ability, Promo and Item cards), complete sets, log in on different days, play the mini-games, and play and win games against the AI: beat every deck of a set, and win against each deck type.
  • Claim only lights up once a mission is complete and there's room under the 12-credit cap for its credits. Gems never block a claim.
  • Claimed missions move to the bottom and grey out.
🏅 Achievements
  • Every card in every set has its own tasks: own 2 copies, win 3 games against each type of AI deck (Mystic, Wind, Neutral, Celestial, Mechanic), win 20 games, attack 5 times with it, and still have it in play at the end of 3 wins.
  • A win only counts for a card you put into play that game: placed during setup, played to the bench, evolved into, or used as an item. Winning on points at turn 30 counts. QA games don't.
  • Items have 7 tasks (no attack and no "still in play" task).
  • 🥉 Bronze, 5 tasks: +1 credit and a 10% chance of a gem. 🥈 Silver, 7 tasks: another credit and a 25% chance of a gem. 🥇 Gold, all 9: a random promo card from the set you choose, at the normal rarity odds.
  • Credit rewards wait until you have room under the 12-credit cap.
🐉 Raid Bosses
  • Each raid boss is built around one card. Its raid opens once you own 2 normal copies of that card.
  • Tasks, counted only in games against that boss: win 5 games in a row, and win once with a deck of at least 14 Mystic creature cards, and the same for Wind, Neutral, Celestial and Mechanic. Losing to the boss or leaving the game resets the streak.
  • The boss deck changes every game: 2 full-art copies of its card, its whole evolution line, and strong cards from the sets you have unlocked.
  • The first clear wins 1 full-art copy of the card. After that the tasks start over, and each new clear pays 1 credit per boss each week (Monday to Sunday). Boss wins also count for the daily win credits.
  • Full-art cards play exactly like the normal ones. They show ✦ in the Card Dex, and the deck builder asks which version you want to add.
💾 Saving
  • Progress saves automatically in this browser.
  • Use Save data → Download backup to keep a file, and Import backup to load it, for example to move your progress between your computer and your phone.
  • Clearing your browser data deletes the browser copy, so keep a recent backup.
🌞 Sundays are special!
  • Promo cards join the draw pool, the only day you can draw them.
  • Spin the Wheel gem prizes are boosted (from +1/+2 up to +3/+6 gems).
  • Guess the Number's and Memory's bonus gem is guaranteed.
