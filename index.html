How to play

Goal

Score 3 points. You score a point each time you knock out one of your opponent's creatures, including creatures on their bench. If neither player reaches 3 points by turn 30, the higher score wins.

Your board

One active creature does the fighting. Up to three bench creatures wait behind it. Retreating swaps the active creature with one from the bench and costs that creature's retreat cost in energy.

Your turn

  • Play one item card
  • Play Stage 1 creatures to empty bench slots
  • Evolve creatures, from turn 3 onwards
  • Attach one energy to any creature you control. Its type (Mystic, Wind, Celestial or Mechanic) is shown on the button before you attach it: one of your deck's energy types, picked at random if the deck uses several
  • Use abilities — these must come before attacking. A creature whose ability is ready has a flashing rainbow border: tap it to see and use the ability
  • Retreat once, or attack, which ends your turn. The creature you retreat into can't be retreated again that turn (moves and abilities that switch creatures still work)

Energy types

Each letter in a move's cost is one energy: M Mystic, W Wind, C Celestial, K Mechanic, and N any type. A cost of MN needs 1 Mystic plus 1 of anything, so a creature holding 2 Celestial energy can't use it. Retreat costs accept any type. Each deck chooses its energy types in the deck builder.

Going first

A coin flip decides who goes first. To make up for moving first, that player does not draw and cannot attack on turn 1. They can still attach energy and play creatures. The other player draws and may attack on their first turn as normal.

Evolution

  • Not before turn 3
  • Not on a creature you played this turn
  • Not twice on the same creature in one turn
  • Damage, energy and status carry over to the new form
  • When an evolved creature is knocked out, every card in its chain goes to the discard pile

Deck building

  • Only cards you own, and no more copies than you own
  • Exactly 20 cards, at most 2 copies of any one card (a 🌟 Mythical card can only be owned once, so 1 copy)
  • At least one Stage 1 creature
  • Creatures of any types can be mixed. Pick the deck's energy types to match: a creature whose moves need an energy type the deck doesn't use gets a warning, but the deck can still be saved
  • Accessory cards are attached to one of your creatures of their type (some fit any creature), one per turn
  • Cards from different sets can be mixed freely

QA mode: choose both decks

Pick any deck for either side, including the AI presets and test decks built from any card. QA games are not counted in stats and never earn credits.

ℹ️ How to play

🎮 What is this game?

Collect creature cards from four sets (Elemental Awakening, Galactic Adventures, Temporal Rift and Crystal Canopy), build decks from the cards you own and battle: against the AI, raid bosses, the daily challenges, or a friend online. Draw cards with credits, buy specific ones in the Store with gems, trade with friends, and earn more credits and gems from wins, missions, achievements and the daily mini-games. The rarest cards, the 🌟 Mythical ones, can only be won from the Mythical raids.

🗂️ What each section does
  • ⚔️ Play: your decks, the deck builder, games against the AI (pick its deck or leave it random), 🎲 Auto team (a random team built from your own cards, also on the Raid Bosses and Daily Challenges pages, following the day's rules) and 🌐 Play online against a friend.
  • 📚 Card Dex: every card in a set and how many of each you own. Tap a card you own for its details, then swipe left or right (or use ‹ ›) to go through your other cards of that set.
  • 🎰 Draw a Card: spend 1 credit for a random card from an unlocked set.
  • 🛒 Store: buy any specific card of an unlocked set with gems. Mythical cards are never sold. Use the arrows at the top to switch to the ✨ Shiny shop, where you swap 10 regular copies of a card and 5 credits for its shiny version.
  • 📋 Missions: objectives that pay credits and gems.
  • 🏅 Achievements: tasks for every single card, with credits, gems and promo cards as rewards.
  • 🐉 Raid Bosses: beat a boss deck to win the full-art version of its card. Mythical raids, opened by completing a set, award a Mythical card.
  • 🔄 Trade: swap one card for one with a friend online. Both pay 5 🪙 and 2 💜, once a day; the cards must be the same rarity, you give one you have 3+ copies of, promos only trade for promos, and nobody gets a card from a set they haven't unlocked.
  • 📅 Daily Challenges: four challenges a day (Easy, Medium, Hard, Extreme) with deck rules to follow, for credits, gems and cards.
  • 🎮 Mini-games: Spin the Wheel, Guess the Number and Memory Game, plus Creature Sorting, Puzzle and Tetris once Temporal Rift is unlocked, and Candy Crush and Snake once Crystal Canopy is. Each is free once a day for credits and gems.
  • 📈 Collection: your progress through each set.
  • 🏆 Stats: games played, won and lost against the AI, each AI deck and raid boss, and your online games and win rate.
  • 💾 Save data: download and import backups, or move your save to another device.
⚔️ How a battle works
  • Goal: score 3 points. You score 1 each time one of your opponent's creatures is knocked out, active or benched. If nobody has 3 by turn 30, the higher score wins (a tie is a draw). A player with no creatures left loses.
  • Board: one active creature does the fighting, with up to 3 on the bench behind it.
  • Setup: you start with 5 cards (always at least one Stage 1). Place a Stage 1 as your active creature, and any others on the bench, then press Ready. A coin flip decides who goes first.
  • Going first: that player doesn't draw and can't attack on turn 1. Everything else is allowed.
  • On your turn: draw a card, then in any order: attach 1 energy to any of your creatures, play Stage 1 creatures to empty bench spots, evolve, play 1 item card, attach 1 accessory, use abilities, and retreat once (pay the retreat cost in energy to swap your active creature with a benched one).
  • Attacking uses one of the active creature's moves and ends your turn, so do everything else first. You can also end your turn without attacking.
  • Evolving: put a Stage 2 on its Stage 1 (or a Stage 3 on its Stage 2). Not before turn 3, not on a creature played this turn, and once per creature per turn. Damage, energy and status conditions carry over; an accessory stays on if the new form is still its type.
  • When your active creature is knocked out, you choose a benched creature to replace it. A knocked-out creature goes to the discard pile with its whole evolution line, energy and accessory (a Vulpetta borrowed with Vulpite just goes back, as it was never in your deck).
  • Some abilities change the rules while their creature is active, for example Chromarion makes your retreats cost 2 more and Astralyon stops your creatures healing. The ✨ chip on the creature explains it.
  • Tap any icon on a creature or under a player's name to see what it means and how long it lasts.
🃏 Types of cards
  • Creatures have HP, a type, a retreat cost and one or two moves. Stage 1 creatures are played directly; Stage 2 evolves from a Stage 1 and Stage 3 from a Stage 2.
  • Some creatures have an ability. Abilities you activate show a flashing rainbow border: tap the creature to use it, once per turn, before attacking. Always-on abilities work by themselves.
  • Item cards have a one-off effect (heal, draw, boost damage...). One item per turn; it goes to the discard pile after use.
  • Accessory cards (Temporal Rift and Crystal Canopy) attach to one of your creatures of their type: one accessory per creature, one per turn. Crystal Canopy's Evo Guard and Core Stabilizer fit any creature. It stays until the creature is knocked out or a move removes it.
  • Promo cards are special cards (mostly creatures; Vulpite is the first promo item): drawn only on Sundays, or won from Gold card achievements. Full-art cards (✦) are won from raid bosses and play exactly like the normal card.
  • ✨ Shiny cards are a sparkling version of the 64 Elemental Awakening creatures (not items or promos). Get one in the Shiny shop for 10 regular copies of that card and 🪙 5 credits; you need 12 regular copies, so you always keep 2. Cards you don't have 12 of are greyed out there, and each tile shows how many of that shiny card you own. A shiny card plays exactly like the normal one.
  • 🌟 Mythical cards (rarity 5) are very strong creatures with an ability and two moves: Astralyon (Celestial), Chromarion (Mechanic) and Aetherave (Mystic). The only way to get one is to clear its Mythical raid, which gives you 1 copy. They can't be drawn, bought, traded or won anywhere else, and you can put your one copy in any deck.
  • Rarities: 🟢 Common · 🔵 Uncommon · ⭐ Rare · 💎 Epic · 🌟 Mythical.
⚡ Energy and move costs
  • Each letter in a cost is one energy: M Mystic, W Wind, C Celestial, K Mechanic, and N any type. "MN" needs 1 Mystic plus 1 of anything.
  • Each deck picks its energy types in the deck builder. With more than one, each turn's energy is one of them at random, and you see which before attaching it.
  • Retreat costs accept energy of any type.
🔥 Types and weaknesses
  • An attack against a type it is strong against deals +10 damage: Mystic beats Wind, Wind beats Celestial, Celestial beats Mystic, and Mechanic beats Neutral.
  • Neutral deals no bonus against anything. While Aurorion's Twist of Fate is in play, every weakness is reversed.
😵 Status conditions

    Only the active creature can have a status condition. It lasts until it is cured (Remedy, some abilities) or the creature leaves the active spot: retreating is the usual way to shake one off.

    Rust and Hex come with Crystal Canopy. Rust only flips on its owner's turn, before they act; if it knocks the creature out, the opponent scores the point. Hex blocks every kind of healing, including Celestial Armor, Evo Echo and heals from items. A creature can have several conditions at once.

    Healing can also be stopped without a status condition: while the opponent's Astralyon is active (Silent Aura), none of your creatures can be healed.

    🧩 Building a deck
    • Exactly 20 cards, with at least one Stage 1 creature.
    • At most 2 copies of any card, and never more copies than you own (normal, full-art and shiny copies together: a shiny or full-art copy still counts towards the 2). If you own more than one version, the deck builder asks which one to add. You can only ever own 1 copy of a Mythical card.
    • Only cards from sets you have unlocked. Types and sets can be mixed freely; a creature whose moves need an energy type your deck doesn't use gets a warning.
    • No time to build one? 🎲 Auto team picks a random 20-card team from your own cards (full art first, never more copies than you own), the way the AI's raid decks are built. In a daily challenge it follows the day's rules.
    • The deck builder filters by set, type, stage, rarity and features (creatures that evolve, with abilities, with move effects, with 2 moves, and their opposites), and searches names, moves and abilities.
    🪙 Currency and draw odds
    • Credits: capped at 12, 1 comes back every hour (even while the game is closed), 1 per card draw.
    • Gems (💜): no cap, start with 5, earned from mini-games, missions, achievements and challenges, spent in the Store.

    Draw odds: 🟢 Common 55% · 🔵 Uncommon 28% · ⭐ Rare 13% · 💎 Epic 4%.

    Store prices: 🟢 Common 1 · 🔵 Uncommon 5 · ⭐ Rare 10 · 💎 Epic 20 gems. 🌟 Mythical cards are never drawn or sold: they only come from the Mythical raids.

    🤖 Games against the AI
    • Choose the AI's deck on the Play page, or leave it random. AI decks from a set join once you've unlocked that set.
    • Each win earns 1 credit, up to 3 a day, shared with online games, raids and daily challenges. If your credits are full or you've had today's 3, the game warns you before you start.
    • Leaving a game before it ends, or closing the page mid-game, counts as a loss.
    • Tap 🎲 Auto team to play the AI with a random team from your own cards; you see it first and can ask for another. Wins with an auto team count for their own missions (5, 10 and 50 wins), in raids and daily challenges too.
    • Your Stats show your win rate going first and going second, and against each AI deck.
    🔒 Unlocking sets

      Promo cards don't count. Until a set is unlocked its cards can't be drawn, bought or used in decks. Once unlocked, choose which set to draw from. Mythical cards aren't a set you unlock: each one is won from its raid.

      📅 Daily Challenges
      • Every day at midnight four new challenges appear: Easy, Medium, Hard and Extreme. Each has its own AI deck, built from strong cards of the sets you have unlocked (the strongest for Extreme), and the same deck all day.
      • Each challenge has rules for your deck, picked at random for the day: for example no cards of a type, no items, only cards from one set, only creatures that evolve, no creatures with 100 HP or more, no creatures with move effects, at least a number of cards of one rarity, a number of creatures with 2 moves, only 1 copy of each card. Easy has 1 rule, Medium 2, and Hard and Extreme 3 tougher ones. Extreme also names one card you own that your deck must include (✅ on the page). The rules always leave a deck you can build from your own cards.
      • Easy needs 1 win. Medium needs 2 wins in a row, Hard 3 and Extreme 4; losing, drawing or leaving resets the streak.
      • Rewards: Easy 🪙 1 credit. Medium 🪙 1 credit and a card (Uncommon or better) from a random unlocked set. Hard 🪙 2 credits, a 50% chance of 💜 1 gem and a card (Uncommon or better) from the set you choose. Extreme the same as Hard (🪙 2 credits and a 50% chance of 💜 1 gem), but the card is Rare or Epic, from the set you choose.
      • 🎲 Auto team on each challenge builds a team from your cards that follows its rules, the must-include card too.
      • Rewards wait on the page until you claim them; the credits need room under the 12-credit cap. Challenge wins also count as normal wins, including the daily win credits.
      🌐 Playing online against a friend
      • On the Play page, tap 🌐 Play online and pick your deck. One player taps Make a room and shares the 4-letter code; the other types it and taps Join.
      • Each turn has 60 seconds. When the time runs out, the turn ends (an open choice is answered for you).
      • A win pays 1 credit, sharing the same limit of 3 win credits a day as games against the AI. Leaving counts as a loss; if the other player leaves or disconnects for long enough, you win.
      • Online games have their own stats (games played, win rate) and their own missions. They don't count towards the card achievements.
      • If the online service is busy or at its free limit, Play online says so instead of starting. If your own connection drops for too long, or the two devices ever disagree about the board, the game stops and doesn't count.
      🐉 Raid Bosses
      • Each raid boss is built around one card. Its raid opens once you own 2 normal copies of that card.
      • Tasks, counted only in games against that boss: win 5 games in a row, and win once with a deck of at least 14 Mystic creature cards, and the same for Wind, Neutral, Celestial and Mechanic. Losing to the boss or leaving the game resets the streak.
      • The boss deck changes every game: 2 full-art copies of its card, its whole evolution line, and strong cards from the sets you have unlocked.
      • The first clear wins 1 full-art copy of the card. After that the tasks start over, and each new clear pays 1 credit per boss each week (Monday to Sunday). Boss wins also count for the daily win credits.
      • Full-art cards play exactly like the normal ones. They show ✦ in the Card Dex, and the deck builder asks which version you want to add.
      • 🎲 Auto team challenges a boss with a random team from your own cards.
      • The page shows one set's bosses at a time: switch sets with the ← → arrows, as in the Card Dex.

      🌟 Mythical raids (the last set on the Raid Bosses page, after Crystal Canopy) are the only way to get a Mythical card:

      • Astralyon opens once you own every Elemental Awakening card (promos too) and 2 copies of every non-promo one. Chromarion needs the same for Galactic Adventures, and Aetherave for Temporal Rift.
      • The boss deck has 2 copies of the Mythical card and only uses cards from the sets up to that one (Astralyon's: Elemental Awakening only; Chromarion's: Elemental Awakening and Galactic Adventures).
      • Tasks: win 7 games in a row; win with at least 14 creature cards of each type, as in other raids; and win with only 1 copy of each card, without creatures of 100 HP or more, without item cards, and with only Common and Uncommon cards.
      • The first clear gives you the Mythical card (1 copy, the only one you can have). After that, clears pay 1 credit a week, like other raids.
      🔄 Trading with a friend
      • On the 🔄 Trade page, one player makes a room and shares the 4-letter code; the other joins with it, like online play.
      • Each of you picks one card to give. You both see the card you give and the card you get, and the trade happens when you both confirm. Changing a card cancels both confirmations.
      • Each trade costs both players 🪙 5 credits and 💜 2 gems, and each player can trade once a day.
      • You can only give a card you own 3 or more normal copies of, both cards must be the same rarity, and nobody can receive a card from a set they haven't unlocked. Promo cards can only be traded for promo cards of the same rarity. Mythical, full-art and shiny cards can't be traded.
      🎮 Mini-games

      Each mini-game is free once a day, and only with 7 or fewer credits (a game can pay up to 5, and credits cap at 12).

      • 🎡 Spin the Wheel: 10 segments, alternating "No Win" and a prize: +1, +3 or +5 credits, or +1/+2 gems.
      • 🎯 Guess the Number: guess a number from 1 to 50. 3 or fewer guesses wins 5 credits, 4-5 wins 3, 6-7 wins 2, 8+ wins 1, plus a 50% chance of a gem. Once you start, you can't leave the page until you guess it.
      • 🧠 Memory Game: find all 8 pairs on the 4×4 board. 50% chance of +2 credits, 40% +3, 10% +5, plus a 50% chance of a gem.
      • 🗄️ Creature Sorting (Temporal Rift): move the creatures between shelves until each shelf holds 3 of the same. Under 0:40: 5 credits and a gem · 1:00: 3 credits and maybe a gem · 1:15: 2 credits · slower: 1.
      • 🧩 Puzzle (Temporal Rift): rebuild a 7×7 full-art picture. 3:00: 5 credits and a gem · 5:00: 3 credits and maybe a gem · 8:00: 2 credits · slower: 1.
      • 🧱 Tetris (Temporal Rift): each cleared line is a point. 20+: 5 credits and a gem · 10+: 3 credits and maybe a gem · 5+: 2 credits · less: 1.
      • 🍬 Candy Crush (Crystal Canopy): level 3, 3 minutes to match candies around blocked cells and cracked rocks. 6,000+ points: 5 credits and a 50% chance of a gem · 5,000+: 3 credits · 3,500+: 2 · less: 1.
      • 🐍 Snake (Crystal Canopy): the small board, edges wrap around. Length 18+: 5 credits and a 50% chance of a gem · 15-17: 3 credits · 10-14: 2 · less: 1.
      • Tetris, Candy Crush and Snake save your score as you play: leaving the page ends the game on the score you had.
      📋 Missions
      • Collect cards (overall, by rarity, by type, with an ability, Promo, Item and Accessory cards), complete and unlock sets, log in on different days, play the mini-games, play and win games against the AI (beat every deck of a set, win against each deck type), win with auto teams, beat raid bosses, and play and win online games.
      • Claim only lights up once a mission is complete and there's room under the 12-credit cap for its credits. Gems never block a claim.
      • Claimed missions move to the bottom and grey out.
      🏅 Achievements
      • Every card in every set, promos included, has its own tasks: own 2 copies (just own it, for a Mythical card), win 3 games against each type of AI deck (Mystic, Wind, Neutral, Celestial, Mechanic), win 20 games, attack 5 times with it, and still have it in play at the end of 3 wins.
      • A win only counts for a card you put into play that game: placed during setup, played to the bench, evolved into, or used as an item. Winning on points at turn 30 counts. QA and online games don't.
      • Items have 7 tasks (no attack and no "still in play" task).
      • 🥉 Bronze, 5 tasks: +1 credit and a 10% chance of a gem. 🥈 Silver, 7 tasks: another credit and a 25% chance of a gem. 🥇 Gold, all 9: a random promo card from the set you choose, at the normal rarity odds.
      • Credit rewards wait until you have room under the 12-credit cap.
      💾 Saving
      • Progress saves automatically in this browser.
      • Use Save data → Download backup to keep a file, and Import backup to load it, for example to move your progress between your computer and your phone. On an iPhone or iPad, Download backup opens the share menu: choose "Save to Files".
      • Clearing your browser data deletes the browser copy, so keep a recent backup. On iPhone, Safari can clear a site's data after a week without a visit; adding the game to the home screen avoids that.
      🌞 Sundays are special!
      • Promo cards join the draw pool, the only day you can draw them.
      • Spin the Wheel gem prizes are boosted (from +1/+2 up to +3/+6 gems).
      • Guess the Number's and Memory's bonus gem is guaranteed.
      • Creature Sorting, Puzzle and Tetris pay at least 1 gem for any finished game, and 2 for the best tier.
      • Candy Crush and Snake: the top tier's gem is guaranteed.
